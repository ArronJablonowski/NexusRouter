## Operational boundary

The feature is available through the built CLI and Go client. Any additive Spark installation is recorded separately after verifying its executable and live API. The existing `nexusrouter-remote.service` binary is not replaced. Caller-controlled workflows still require trusted nodes and explicit capacity planning. Uncertain deliveries require operator reconciliation and cannot be blindly retried.
