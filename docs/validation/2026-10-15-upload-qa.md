## Findings and dispositions

All reproductions use synthetic fixtures; no real credentials or unrelated document contents were inspected or published. Original source files/downloads were never rewritten.

| ID | Reproduced issue | Verified source fix |
|---|---|---|
| UQA-01 | On a PDF page with an image, OCR replaced already-searchable text. Empty or imperfect recognition could lose exact native content. | Preserve native text in its own labeled block alongside OCR; keep helper failures fail-closed. | UQA-02 | JSON escaping made a legal 64 KiB HTML/text attachment exceed the decoder's 256 KiB frame ceiling, bypassing attachment parsing/image conversion. | Separate 1 MiB encoded-frame ceiling from 64 KiB/file and 128 KiB combined decoded bounds; test escaped-text round trips and both HTTP image adapters. | UQA-03 | Bytewise redaction missed JSON-escaped configured credentials in attachment text and could damage image base64 matching a configured secret string. Redundant history screening then hid the chat. | Decode and scrub text/name fields, preserve binary inference inputs, reject over-limit redaction expansion, and provide text-only browser/classifier views without image base64. Covers preparation, journal persistence, saved history and classifier input. Original private downloads remain untouched. |
| UQA-04 | Transparent black graphics became black-on-black when flattened into JPEG; dark original previews were also illegible. | Composite analysis thumbnails on white and give previews a white background; preserve original transparency in stored/downloaded bytes. Pixel contrast and real Chrome computed-style tests added. |
| UQA-05 | DOCX explicit `w:br`/`w:cr` line breaks were omitted, joining adjacent text. | Preserve explicit breaks alongside existing paragraph/tab separators. Regression and real DOCX corpus verify exact newlines. |
| UQA-06 | Picker completion during a temporary composer-disable refresh silently discarded the selected files. | Accept explicitly chosen files into the local draft even while composition is blocked. Send controls, frozen request identities and backend permissions remain unchanged; no automatic submission is introduced. |

No evidence of real credential exposure was found or sought; this is synthetic defect verification.
